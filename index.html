FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TideBites?

An online restaurant for campus life, with comforting meals, refreshing treats, and a playful splash of summer.

Can I visit a physical restaurant?

TideBites is online-only and has no physical storefront.

Can I order here?

Yes! Browse the menu, add dishes to your cart, fill in your name and phone number, and place your order. Orders are submitted to our system and delivery will be arranged at your chosen campus location.

How does the student deal work?

Every campus order automatically gets 10% off all meals and desserts. No student ID, account, or promo code is needed. The discount is calculated on the full food subtotal and rounded to the nearest NT dollar.

Do you have desserts?

Yes! Discover Watermelon Snow Ice, Mango Snow Ice, and Peanut Tang Yuan.

Can I filter dishes or enlarge photos?

Yes! Choose ALL, MEALS, or DESSERTS and browse up to six dishes per page. Open a dish for details, then select the magnifying glass to enlarge its photo.
