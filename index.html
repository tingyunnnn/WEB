Student Discount

We love supporting students! Show your valid student ID and enjoy exclusive benefits every time you order.

10% Off

All university students get 10% off every order, every day.

How to Claim

Simply show your valid student ID when ordering to redeem your discount.

Valid All Year

No expiry, no limits. Your discount is valid every day, all year round.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible?

Any student currently enrolled in a university or college with a valid student ID card.

How much is the discount?

10% off all items on our menu — food, desserts, and drinks included!

Can I combine with other offers?

Yes! The student discount can be used together with our daily specials and seasonal promotions.

Is there a minimum spend?

No minimum spend required. The discount applies to any order, big or small.

How often can I use it?

As many times as you like! The discount is valid for every visit throughout the year.

What if I forget my student ID?

A digital copy of your student ID on your phone is also accepted.
